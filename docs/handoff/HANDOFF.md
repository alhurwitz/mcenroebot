## 1. What the machine is

A hobby ping-pong **ball feeder** ("V4"): a spin/aim ball machine that serves 40 mm balls at
controlled speed and spin to chosen court locations, with a closed recycle loop —
**catch net → trough → auger → hopper → feed airlock → launch wheels**.

There is **no incoming-ball tracking, no strike-plane prediction, no real-time control loop**. It is a
periodic scheduler driving actuators. (Vision, added later, is for *scoring and coaching*, not for
returning balls.)

**Shelved:** the earlier V2 "aim-and-swat" rally stack (`ball/`, `predictor/`, `swing/`,
`RallyCoordinator`) lives under `src/mcenroebot/_shelved/` and is not imported by the feeder.

**Long-term cosmetic goal:** the working machine gets dressed as a McEnroe caricature (sculpted
shell, camera eyes, speaker mouth, trash-talking voice). **Shell work is explicitly deferred until
shots work on the bench** (AJ, 2026-07-04).

## 4. Decision log — what was chosen and why

### Aiming architecture (2026-07-04, revised 2026-07-09)

- **Base pans; the launcher tilts.** Tilt pivots about the wheel axle, range **−5°…+30°**.
- **Why tilt at all:** `tilt_envelope.py` (40 mm/2.7 g drag model; Cowork `scripts/`, not yet in the
  repo) shows range is drag-limited
  to ~13–15 m and **saturates by 15–20°** — tilt buys **lob apex** (0.8 m flat → 2.1 m at 30°), not
  range. It also compensates the weaker backspin side.
- **Net clearance:** a 0.8 m launch axis is *below* a 0.914 m tennis net → 0° tilt cannot clear it;
  ≥10° clears, 20° clears by 0.4–1.9 m.
- **Pan the platform, keep the auger + collection ground-fixed** — otherwise the auger intake swings
  away from the net's fixed drop slot. Ball handoff happens at the pan axis. *(Still valid; the
  parts that implemented it, `pan_base_v1` + `launcher_tilt_v1`, are obsolete.)*

### Launcher: tri-wheel (2026-07-04) — current

- **3× 60 mm wheels at 120°** about the fire axis (bottom + upper-right + upper-left), centers 48.5
  from axis, 1.5 mm squeeze each. AJ's call: "three wheel mechanism will work better than 1 on top
  1 on bottom." Supersedes the 2-wheel v3 bracket.
- **Feed path:** rear drop chute → **quarter-pipe slide** (r42 trough; replaced a 40° shoe at AJ's
  request — "angle into feed like a slide", no kinks) → axial Ø46 tube through a rear bulkhead →
  bottom tire pulls the ball into the throat. No top wheel ⇒ no corridor dodge, no nip wedge.
- **Mixing:** mean of all three = exit speed; upper-pair differential ≈ **sidespin**; bottom vs upper
  mean = **top/backspin**.
- Bottom module keeps a v3-style flat plate so the old `launcher_bench_dock_v1` (+ 0/10/20/30°
  wedges) still fits — though the dock fit poorly for AJ and he props/clamps instead. **Don't push a
  dock v2 unless he asks.**
- **Files:** `3d-models/launcher_bracket_v4_tri/` (Cowork folder).

### Launcher tuning facts (bench, 2026-07-10)

- Speed is **massively surplus** — a single 1000KV fired balls "really fast" at ~20% throttle. The
  hard problem is **low-end resolution just above the ESC deadband**, where most shots live.
- First tri-wheel firing: base throttle 0.30 was "wayyyy too fast" → defaults retuned to
  **base 0.12 / diff 0.03 / 0.01 steps**. Useful window ≈ **0.10–0.20**. At low base keep diff small
  or the slow wheel of a spin preset stalls below its floor.
- **Deadband drifts with battery charge** (open-loop ESCs; floor and rpm scale with pack V and creep
  up as the pack sags). Measured floors on a fresh-ish pack: **A=0.050, B=0.045, BOT=0.045** —
  well below the assumed ~9%.
- **Ch4's ESC kept spinning at commanded zero** (its calibrated min is below 1000 µs). Mitigation in
  script: on exit, zero throttle → 0.4 s latch → cut PWM entirely so the ESC failsafes off. The real
  fix is recalibrating ch4's throttle range (wizard is in the TUI, `c` key).
- Tool: **`scripts/triwheel_spin_test.py`** — Textual dashboard; arms all 3 ESCs, presets
  FLAT / TOPSPIN / BACKSPIN / 2× sidespin, per-wheel trims, floor-hunt mode (`d`), guided ESC
  calibration (`c`), `f` = fire via feed servo, E-STOP. (`textual` is now a declared dependency —
  commit 7a17de7 — so it runs from a clean `uv sync`.)
- Unsolved: the bench **rattles a lot at speed** (wheel balance? bracket resonance?). Sidespin preset
  4's direction is unverified — label it after a bench run.
- Candidate permanent fix (not built): **INA219** on the shared I2C bus → live voltage-scaled throttle
  in `ThrottleMap`.

### Feed airlock — **COMPLETE on the bench** (2026-07-04)

- **Positional MG996R replaced the continuous HD3512MG-360.** Why: a positional servo *holds* the
  commanded LOAD/DISCH angles, so hard end-stops (`scoop_endstops_v1`) are unnecessary, there's no
  stall heat, and it reuses the existing positional servo stack. Torque was never the issue (3 g
  balls; the hopper neck-down carries the pile weight off the scoop).
- **Bench-set angles: LOAD 70° / DISCH 175°** (105° rock, dwell 0.65 s) on the v3 arm. Note DISCH is
  only 5° off the 180° limit — little headroom. Baked into `scripts/feed_meter.py` defaults.
- **Servo wall v2:** the original thin 5 mm wall snapped off and its holes were wrong. Replacement
  `feed_servo_wall_v2/` = 6 mm solid plate + thick foot flange + 4 M3 **slots** (absorb clone
  variance); final coaxiality is set with an arm jig, not by nominal dims.
- **Double-feed → solved by the ring-stopper arm.** A 2nd ball dropped through at ~70° into the
  swing (it rode on the cup back at LOAD and lost support). AJ's fix, taken all the way: a **full
  ring band** (r54–60.5, narrowed to Y9–29, ~292° arc, cup opening as the only gap, 3 spokes) that
  shadows the chute mouth whichever way the arm rocks — kills the side/mirror guessing.
  **`scoop_arm_cup26_v3/` is the arm of record.** (v2's one-sided sector is the lighter alternative
  still on disk.)
- Result: **one ball per rock, clean release, no double-feed.**

### Auger lift

- `auger_lift_v2/` is current. Built on **AJ's own honeycomb tube segments** (`tube_segment_hex_FINAL`:
  160 tall, OD114 / ID101.6, socket-bottom / spigot-top) + 3-turn screw segments (flight OD97,
  pitch 51.2). Shaft forced ≤Ø21 by ID101.6 + a 40 mm ball (radial clearance 40.8 — tight).
- **Stack:** motor_mount + 4× plain tube + discharge_segment; screw = drive + 4× stack + top pin.
  **658 mm (2.16 ft) of lift at a 60° incline.**
- **60° incline confirmed** by AJ as the reliable minimum lean — side-window discharge needs the 0.5 g
  radial nudge; vertical would require a top-overflow discharge instead. It's a one-line param.
- **Print screws on their side** — flat-flight vertical printing = 24 h with supports; sideways = 6 h
  and the grain runs strong for torque. A **self-supporting 45° coned screw is impossible** at this
  pitch (it chokes the channel from 47 mm to ~25 mm and blocks the 40 mm ball) — *don't re-offer it.*
- ⚠️ **Open ambiguity — which drive?** `channel_map.py` and the base redesign assume the **GB37RG gear
  motor + L298N on GPIO18**. `auger_lift_v2/PRINT_LIST.md` describes a **continuous-servo** drive and
  calls the GB37RG parts a shelved fallback. Both part sets exist on disk. **Confirm with AJ before
  printing.** (Gear motor is the safer choice: a continuous servo has no current limit and will grind
  itself against a jam.)
- **Not yet built:** the top hopper + guide from the discharge window (AJ asked for "both: hopper w/
  guide"). Auger dumps into hopper → hopper gravity-feeds the feed unit.
- Torso note: scaffold's `AUGER_OD` placeholder is **110**; the real tube is **114** — fix at
  integration.

### Stand / aiming hardware — `shooter_stand_v2_pan` is current

AJ's reaction to the 17-part `shooter_stand_v1`: *"feels like a HUGE job… I'm just looking to shoot
balls left and right over the net — design a stand and future-proof it so we can add tilt/pan."*
Result: a **5-part staged stand**.

| Stage | Contents |
|---|---|
| **1 (now, no servo)** | Turntable deck (integral **fixed 15° cradle**, both-face plate grip + clamp screws) + riser lower/upper (feed platform) + 3 bolt-on feet. Aim by turning the stand. 15° clears the net. |
| **2** | Print `base_drum`, unbolt the feet, drop the **same deck** on it: MG996R direct-drive pan, thrust ridge r100–107 carries the load, ±90°. Deck height is identical, so **nothing re-tunes**. |
| **3** | `shooter_stand_v1` parts = full servo pan + −5…+30° tilt (17 parts, 2× MG996R, ring gear 105T/44T pan, 80T/16T sector tilt, ~1.0–1.3 kg counterweight TBD). |

- Rev B (AJ's review): hopper (`hopper_neckdown_v1`, Ø165, ~0.4 kg) rides the feed-chute top; whole
  placement shifted −30 mm in y so CG lands at r84 < ridge r100. Riser moved onto a deck tab to stop
  it colliding with the tilted launcher chute.
- **Rev C (gussets + knee braces) was REVERTED** at AJ's request 2026-07-10 — *"that didn't work, I
  can always add another support."* Current STLs are plain rev-B risers. If the platform shivers on
  the bench, the known escalation is a diagonal strut deck→platform-end via the clear corridor at
  x ≈ −40.
- **Coupler telescope ≈ 101 mm** flange-to-flange, in every stage.

### Vision (2026-07-11) — for scoring/coaching, not for returning balls

- `events.py` thresholds are **image-space px/s** — they must be re-tuned whenever the camera moves.
- **Next piece:** the scorer state machine (~50 lines) over the event stream. In/out calls need court

- `camera_mount_v1/` (repo, 12c3cc9): shell back/front + mast, ±35° friction tilt, mounts to the deck

### Shell / cosmetics (deferred until shots work)

- `mcenroe_shell_v6/` is current, from AJ's image-to-3D sculpt (no legs — base goes where legs were).
  Head reverted to **natural sculpt size (~208 mm)** after 155 mm was "way too small"; figure ~810 mm.
- **Approach chosen: slip-over cosmetic cover**, not structural — 2.5 mm uniform wall, open bottom,
  openings for belly port / rear auger / neck. **14 watertight pieces ≤248 mm** in

- Structure comes from `torso_scaffold_v3` instead: 3 internal plates screwed **from outside through
  the shell wall** (18× M3, plate z 149/253/367). Full spec in
  [`reference/design/SHELL_HANDOFF.md`](reference/design/SHELL_HANDOFF.md).
- **Eyes** (the recurring bug): eye line source-z ≈ 35.2, pupils x ≈ −4.8 / +2.8 (~58 mm IPD). The
  camera bar is 13.2×80×16 mm spanning both eyes. The painter's-matplotlib renderer shows a **false
  dark eye-band on hollow shells** — verify holes with the open-map raster or sections, not that
  renderer.

### Software conventions (enforced — see `CLAUDE.md`)

- Every component under `src/mcenroebot/` is a **package** (dir + `__init__.py`), never a flat module.
  Tests mirror 1:1 under `tests/test_<name>/`.
- **All value objects are frozen pydantic v2 models**, keyword-only constructors, validation in
  `@field_validator`. Not dataclasses. Validation failures raise `ValidationError`.
- Controllers are **stateless apart from injected config**; unreachable targets return `None`, never
  raise.
- Every GPIO-touching driver = `Protocol` + real impl + `Mock<Name>Driver`. Higher layers are tested
  against mocks only; real drivers are `@pytest.mark.integration`, Pi-only. Pi deps import **lazily**.
- Coordinates: origin at the yaw axis, **+X forward, +Y left, +Z up**; meters, degrees for servo I/O.
- `uv` for everything (`uv add`, never hand-edit `pyproject.toml`). Coverage floor **85%**, mypy
  strict, ruff, conventional commits (commitizen), gitflow (`main`/`develop`/`feature/*`).
- Python **3.13** runtime; `pyproject.toml` still declares `>=3.11` and mypy/ruff target `py311` —
  intentional, out-of-scope drift.
